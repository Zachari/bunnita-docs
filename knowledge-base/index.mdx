## Contested facts

Entries carry a `## Contested` section rather than silently resolving a disagreement between sources. The full set is listed here so it is visible without opening every entry, including the items now resolved — kept in the table so their provenance stays visible. **Recording a contradiction is not resolving it** — rulings belong to Zachari or a ratified committee proposal.

**Four of the eleven items below remain open** — #2, #8, #9 and #10, none of them blocking. Seven were resolved by convener direction on 2026-09-14 (#1, #3, #4, #5, #6, #7, #11): five in the document-truth remediation pass that followed the Round 30 committee audit, #4 by the governance amendment that followed it, and #6 by the ceiling ruling. Where an entry's disagreement was resolved, its `## Contested` section was rewritten as a `## Resolved` record rather than deleted, so the provenance of the original divergence survives.

**Recording a resolution here is not propagating it, and the two are separate steps.** #7 was ruled resolved in this table on 2026-09-14 while four canonical or project locations kept the old count, including the entry this table named as its home — the same failure #6 had one release earlier, where one convener grant was executed in a new build and nine other documents kept 6. A resolution is closed only when every location that _states_ the value is amended, and a row here carries the propagation record so that closure is auditable rather than asserted. #7's propagation is recorded in its row.

**No gating items remain.** Both blockers closed on 2026-09-14. #4 defined the six-member vote mechanism (4 of 6 carries; 3–3 escalates to the convener), restoring the committee vote as a working ratification channel. #6 ratified the extension ceiling at 7 by convener ruling, which was the only available channel because that rule is vote-proof by construction. The four items still listed below are ordinary open questions, ratifiable by a carried vote plus convener approval — no longer blocked on a broken mechanism.

## Rules

- Exactly one entry per fact carries `authority: canonical`. Everything else is `reference` and links to it.
- An entry's one-line summary here must match its content. Drift between summary and content fails the consistency audit.
- Entries change only on Zachari's request or a ratified committee proposal with reasoning — the immutability rule, now enforceable per-entry rather than per-monolith.
- An entry listed here must resolve on disk. A row pointing at a missing file fails the audit.
