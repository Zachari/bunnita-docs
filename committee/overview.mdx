The Bunnita Committee is the governance engine behind every architectural decision in the framework. Before a new capability, constraint, or structural change reaches a build, it passes through a full deliberation cycle — six specialized agents pitching positions, issuing rebuttals, and casting recorded votes. Nothing is decided in silence, and nothing is decided alone.

## The Six-Agent Committee

The committee comprises six specialized AI agents, each owning a distinct domain of the Bunnita architecture. They debate every significant decision, vote on outcomes, and record the full deliberation — positions, rebuttals, concessions, and all — in a permanent, append-only governance journal.

**Zachari** convenes the committee and holds final architectural authority. A vote that carries makes a selection *eligible*, but Zachari's explicit approval is required before anything becomes the focus of the next build.

## Expansion in v1.6 (Rounds 26+)

The committee grew from four members to six during the v1.6 cycle. **Søren Aalto** and **Renata Ferreira** joined to establish the engineering-and-innovation pillar — a dimension the original four never owned.

Their addition was deliberate by design: Søren and Renata operate as a counter-pair.

- **Søren** proposes — whole-system design, target-model fit, capability-native inversions
- **Renata** proves — seam analysis, referential integrity, empirical ship gates

Neither ships an engineering decision alone. Søren's vision requires Renata's verification; Renata's rigor requires Søren's scope. The counter-pair structure prevents both unchecked ambition and paralytic caution.

## Vote Arithmetic

The committee uses a straightforward majority model with strict dissent documentation.

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| **6–0** unanimous | Carries — all agreement recorded |
| **5–1** or **4–2** | Carries — every dissenting position recorded in full |
| **3–3** split | Does not carry — both sides compiled as a steel-manned brief and escalated to Zachari |

A carried vote makes a selection *eligible*. It does not scope the selection into the next build. Zachari reviews the result and the deliberation record before assigning build focus.

## The Governance Journal

Every deliberation is permanently logged in an append-only governance journal at `Subagents/committee-deliberation.md`. The journal records member positions, pitches, rebuttals, vote results, and all dissent — attributed, in each agent's own voice. No round is ever edited or removed. The record exists to make the reasoning behind every architectural decision auditable in perpetuity.
